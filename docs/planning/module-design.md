# Module Design

## Project

BoutiqueIQ – AI-Powered Clothing Inventory & Catalog Management Platform for Boutiques

---

# Overview

The BoutiqueIQ application is divided into multiple functional modules. Each module performs a specific business operation while interacting with other modules through the FastAPI backend.

---

# Modules

## 1. Authentication Module

Responsibilities

- Login
- Logout
- JWT Authentication
- Role-Based Authorization

---

## 2. Dashboard Module

Responsibilities

- Business Summary
- Revenue Statistics
- Low Stock Alerts
- AI Prediction Summary

---

## 3. Product Management Module

Responsibilities

- Add Product
- Update Product
- Delete Product
- Product Images
- SKU Management

---

## 4. Category Management Module

Responsibilities

- Create Category
- Update Category
- Delete Category

---

## 5. Inventory Management Module

Responsibilities

- Stock In
- Stock Out
- Inventory Tracking
- Stock Transactions

---

## 6. Supplier Management Module

Responsibilities

- Supplier Registration
- Purchase Orders
- Supplier Information

---

## 7. Customer Management Module

Responsibilities

- Customer Records
- Customer History

---

## 8. Sales Management Module

Responsibilities

- Sales Invoice
- Billing
- Returns
- Payment Records

---

## 9. AI Prediction Module

Responsibilities

- Demand Forecasting
- Inventory Optimization
- Stock Recommendation

---

## 10. Report Module

Responsibilities

- Sales Report
- Inventory Report
- Purchase Report
- Forecast Report

---

# Conclusion

The modular architecture improves scalability, maintainability, and future expansion of the BoutiqueIQ platform.
