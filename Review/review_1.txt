1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
============================================================

Project Name:
BoutiqueIQ

Project Description:
BoutiqueIQ is an AI-powered clothing inventory and catalog management
platform designed for boutique businesses.

The system is intended to help boutique owners manage:
- Products
- Inventory
- Suppliers
- Customers
- Sales
- Purchase operations
- Reports
- AI-powered business insights

Long-term AI goals:
- Demand Forecasting
- Inventory Optimization
- Stock Recommendation

Current focus:
The current development phase focuses on building the core backend
and business modules required to support these future AI features.


============================================================
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
============================================================

Boutique owners may manage products, inventory, suppliers, customers,
and sales manually or through disconnected systems. This can result in
inaccurate stock information, difficulty tracking business operations,
and limited ability to make data-driven decisions.

BoutiqueIQ aims to provide a centralized platform for managing these
operations and later use Artificial Intelligence for demand forecasting
and inventory optimization.

11. IMPORTANT TECHNICAL EXPLANATIONS
============================================================

Q: Why FastAPI?

A:
FastAPI was selected for high-performance REST API development,
automatic OpenAPI/Swagger documentation, dependency injection, and
easy integration with SQLAlchemy and PostgreSQL.

Q: Why PostgreSQL?

A:
PostgreSQL is a reliable relational database suitable for structured
business data such as products, customers, suppliers, inventory, and
future sales transactions.

Q: Why SQLAlchemy?

A:
SQLAlchemy provides ORM functionality. It allows the backend to work
with database tables through Python models and supports relationships
between entities.

Q: Why JWT?

A:
JWT provides stateless authentication. After successful login, the
backend generates a token and protected APIs require that token.

Q: Why is AI not completed yet?

A:
The current phase focuses on building the core transactional modules.
The AI demand forecasting system requires structured historical sales
and inventory data. Therefore, the core business modules are being
implemented first. The AI module will be developed after sufficient
business data and sales functionality are available.
